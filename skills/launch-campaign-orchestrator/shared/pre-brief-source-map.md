# Pre-Brief Source Map

Use this file as the source-priority contract for `Phase: Pre-brief Search` in launch campaign communication briefs (上市传播、新车上市、launch narrative).

## Usage Rules

- Pre-brief search is for signal discovery, not final narrative or strategy claims.
- Prefer source mix over single-platform conclusions.
- Align provisional takeaways with `./shared/source-trust-policy.md`: discovery passes may lean on Medium/Low for **hints only**; anything that becomes a factual claim later must be rebuilt as Atomic Resources under Insight with explicit trust tiers (Low → Insight re-validation, never Proposal core claims alone).
- If sources disagree, preserve the conflict as an intake confirmation item.

## Source Buckets

### Social Platform And Creator Signals

- priority: first
- best_for: topic heat, audience language, creator formats, meme or visual trends, launch-adjacent conversation
- trust_level: medium for discovery; low when single-thread or unsubstantiated
- use_when: brief emphasizes burst, feeds, creators, youth or lifestyle narratives
- example_sources: 小红书话题与笔记, Douyin/Bilibili creators, Weibo hashtags, Kuaishou short video (品类相关时选用)

### Local Life And Venue Or Event Signals

- priority: second
- best_for: city rollout timing, footprint traffic, experiential or OOH feasibility, festival or holiday overlays
- trust_level: medium
- use_when: brief depends on geography, experiential stunts, local PR, or event tie-ins
- example_sources: 本地生活平台, city lifestyle media, mall or district calendars, municipal event listings (verify before hard claims)

### Business And Industry Media

- priority: third
- best_for: brand moves, category launches, competitor campaign patterns, narratives that need editorial grounding
- trust_level: medium to high depending on outlet
- use_when: social noise needs calibration or comparative launch context is missing
- example_sources: 36Kr, 虎嗅, 界面, 第一财经, LatePost — pick by category relevance

### Official And Public Data

- priority: fourth
- best_for: compliance-adjacent baselines, category stats, registrations or sales proxies where public, transit or tourism overlays for major cities
- trust_level: high when primary official releases
- use_when: a proposed angle hinges on stakes, timing windows, or "why now" narratives that need durable backing
- example_sources: 部委/行业协会发布, brand IR or official newsroom, audited summaries, local statistics portals

## Selection Heuristics

- If the brief emphasizes **social-led launch burst**, start with social/creator buckets, then cross-check key threads with industry media or official anchors.
- If the brief emphasizes **local activation or spectacle**, elevate local/event signals earlier and use official calendars for date risk checks.
- If a signal is high-noise (viral rumor, ambiguous leak), mark as provisional and queue for Insight re-validation alongside Atomic Resources.
