# Conversion Mechanics Playbook

Use this contract in `Phase: Action` for order conversion design and dealer close-rate enablement.

## Promotional Mechanics Catalog

### Price mechanics (4)

#### Price mechanic usage rule

- Every price mechanic must include eligibility and expiry.

1. limited-time cash discount
2. trade-in bonus
3. insurance subsidy bundle
4. accessory value pack

### Experience mechanics (4)

#### Experience mechanic usage rule

- Experience design must include attendance-to-test-drive linkage.

1. family scenario test-drive route
2. weekend micro event with booking slots
3. launch-day fast-track consultation
4. owner testimony conversion session

### Finance mechanics (4)

#### Finance mechanic usage rule

- Finance communication must disclose key terms in plain language.

1. low down-payment option
2. low-interest installment
3. deferred payment window
4. monthly-payment cap package

### Selection rules

- Match persona, budget pressure, and policy window.
- Use max 2 core mechanisms per card to avoid message dilution.
- Every mechanism needs cost, eligibility, and expiry rules.

## Urgency and Scarcity Design Patterns

### Trigger patterns (5)

#### Authenticity rule

- Trigger claims must be traceable to inventory, policy, or schedule constraints.

1. slot scarcity (test-drive / consultation capacity)
2. time-window scarcity (N-day / weekend-only)
3. policy-window urgency (subsidy deadline)
4. inventory urgency (configured stock limit)
5. privilege urgency (early-order bundle)

### Integrity rule

- Every urgency claim must map to real operational constraints; no fake countdown.

## Conversion Event vs Launch Event Design

### 8-dimension difference table

| Dimension | Launch event | Conversion event |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary goal | awareness | order close |
| Core KPI | reach/voice | order/deposit |
| Audience mix | broad | high-intent leads |
| Content shape | narrative and showcase | objection handling and offer matching |
| Duration | staged and ceremonial | short-cycle and repeated |
| CTA intensity | medium | high |
| Sales staffing | support | frontline-heavy |
| Follow-up clock | day-level | hour-level |

### Conversion event template

- event objective
- target lead tier mix
- selected mechanics
- objection checklist
- close-path script
- follow-up owner and SLA

### Low-cost preferred conversion forms

- 48-hour deep test-drive in real family scenarios.
- targeted conversion test-drive sessions (legacy owners, enterprise cohorts, community groups).
- lightweight policy and financing consultation corners (no heavy stage build).

## Test-Drive-to-Order Conversion Path

### 6-step funnel

#### Funnel ownership rule

- Each step must define a dealer owner role and expected completion signal.

1. lead confirmation
2. test-drive booking
3. on-site reception and profiling
4. scenario drive and objection capture
5. offer matching and deposit push
6. order confirmation and post-order handoff

### Branch handling

- no-show branch: 24h re-booking flow.
- price objection branch: policy or finance re-match.
- family decision delay branch: follow-up toolkit for co-decision members.

## Dealer Sales Enablement

### Required enablement pack

#### Pack maintenance cadence

- Refresh at least weekly during launch burst.

- unified talk track by lead tier
- competitor FAQ with response examples
- regional policy and subsidy knowledge table

### Minimum daily drill

- one policy refresh
- one competitor objection role-play
- one close-path replay

## Deposit Follow-Up SOP

### 6 touchpoints from deposit to delivery

#### Touchpoint record minimum

- timestamp, owner, status, and next action.

1. D0 deposit confirmation and expectation setting
2. D1 policy and document checklist
3. D3 order status sync
4. D7 experience content and referral teaser
5. pre-delivery readiness check
6. delivery-day activation and community invite

### Cancellation-risk intervention

- risk signals: delayed response, repeated price hesitation, financing uncertainty.
- intervention: senior callback, alternate package, delivery certainty clarification.

## Regional Policy and Subsidy Linkage

### Verification checklist

#### Policy source requirement

- Policy item must include source and validity date.

- policy validity date
- eligibility boundaries
- required documents
- dealer execution owner

### Cost-transparency template

- guide price
- policy deduction
- finance cost
- accessory/service additions
- final monthly and total ownership estimate

## Gate for Action use

- Fail if promotional catalog and selection rule are missing.
- Fail if test-drive-to-order 6-step path is incomplete.
- Fail if deposit follow-up SOP and policy-linkage checklist are missing.
