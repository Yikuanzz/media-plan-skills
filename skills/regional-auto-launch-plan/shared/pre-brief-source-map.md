# Pre-Brief Source Map

Use this file as the source-priority contract for `Phase: Pre-brief Search` in regional auto launch briefs.

## Usage Rules

- Pre-brief search is for signal discovery, not final strategy claims.
- Prefer source mix over single-platform conclusions.
- For local launch timing and venue topics, add city-level official/event sources early.
- If sources disagree, preserve the conflict as an intake confirmation item.

## Source Buckets

### Auto Platform And Community Signals

- priority: first
- best_for: model discussion heat, user pain points, creator topic direction, short-term sentiment
- trust_level: medium for discovery, low when unsupported
- use_when: need fast signal discovery around model positioning, test-drive intent, or creator conversation
- example_sources: Dongchedi, Autohome forums, Xiaohongshu auto posts, Douyin auto creators, Weibo auto topics, Bilibili auto reviews

### Local City And Lifestyle Signals

- priority: second
- best_for: district-level consumption heat, local event atmosphere, weekend and holiday behavior
- trust_level: medium
- use_when: brief depends on city scenes, local lifestyle narratives, or offline attendance pull
- example_sources: city media, local lifestyle accounts, commercial district reports, local event calendars

### Business And Industry Media

- priority: third
- best_for: brand moves, model launch context, regional market competition, dealer dynamics
- trust_level: medium to high
- use_when: platform chatter needs editorial calibration or competitor context
- example_sources: 36Kr, Huxiu, Jiemian, Yicai, Caixin, LatePost (when relevant)

### Official And Public Data

- priority: fourth
- best_for: policy windows, registration/sales baselines, traffic and tourism flows, macro consumption baselines
- trust_level: high
- use_when: key claims require stable baseline or policy validation
- example_sources: local commerce bureaus, local statistics bureaus, transportation bureaus, Ministry-level releases, industry associations

## Selection Heuristics

- If the brief emphasizes launch burst and social spread, start with auto platform/community signals, then validate with media and official data.
- If the brief emphasizes local activation, move city and official/event sources earlier.
- If a signal is high-noise (viral topic, rumor-like claim), mark as provisional and queue for Insight re-validation.
